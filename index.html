How the reminder works

Your suggested leave time is calculated from the live tram arrival estimate, walking time, preparation time and an extra buffer.

Example

If a suitable tram is due in 20 minutes, your stop is 5 minutes away and you want a 3-minute buffer, the suggested leave time is in 12 minutes. If you also need 15 minutes to prepare, that tram is not realistically catchable and we look for a later one.

For genuine Alexa or phone notifications when this page is closed, a scheduled backend and permissions would be needed.
